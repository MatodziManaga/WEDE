About Ubuntu Paws Rescue

We are a fictional non-profit, created for this project, that shows how a real animal rescue could present itself online.

Who we are

Ubuntu Paws Rescue began in 2014 when a group of neighbours in Johannesburg started sheltering stray dogs in a garage. Today we run two sites, a sterilisation clinic and a network of foster homes.

To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned animals, and to reduce suffering through sterilisation and education.

A Johannesburg where every pet is wanted, healthy and safe, and no animal is born unwanted.
